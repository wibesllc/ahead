Ahead Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Ahead is built to be private by default. Ahead has no accounts and no backend. The app never sends your data anywhere, and we never receive, store, sell, or share it. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The one thing worth understanding is Send a copy, because it is the only feature that puts your information into something that leaves your phone. It is described in full under Sharing below.

What the App Stores Locally

The application stores the following information locally, on your device only:

  • Tasks, to-dos, appointments, and daily routines
  • Reusable checklists
  • Service provider contact details
  • Your profile name and reminder preferences

The app itself makes no network requests. It has no accounts, no sync and no backend, and it never uploads anything you enter. The single exception to data leaving your phone is a share link you create yourself, described next.

Sharing ("Send a copy")

When you choose Send a copy on a list or a task, Ahead creates a link and hands it to whatever app you pick (Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, AirDrop, and so on). We are not involved in that: the message goes from your phone to your recipient through the app you chose.

The shared item's contents travel inside the link itself. There is no upload and no copy on any server of ours, which is why a shared link keeps working with no account and no internet service behind it. It also means two things you should know:

  • Anyone who has the link can open what you shared. Treat it like the message it is in. There is no way to revoke a link once it is sent, and there is no password on it.
  • Our website can see the contents in two situations. The link points at wibesllc.com. If your recipient already has Ahead installed, tapping it opens the app directly and no request reaches our website at all. But if they do not have Ahead, or if their messaging app fetches a preview of the link, then the web address, which contains the shared item, reaches our website's server. It appears there only in ordinary web server access logs, kept briefly by our hosting provider. We do not read them for this purpose, do not store shared items in any database, and do not use them for anything.

Only what you chose to share is included. Ahead deliberately leaves out your reminder settings, your calendar links, your completion history, and your saved service provider details when it builds a share.

If you would rather share nothing over a link, use Export or import data in Profile → Privacy & Data to save a file instead, and send that file however you like.

What We Don't Do

Ahead does not perform analytics collection, does not include advertising, does not require an account, and does not access device hardware such as your microphone or camera. The app does not upload, sync, or back up your data to any server of ours. We build no profile of you, and we have no way to connect a share link to the person who created it.

Permission Usage

Notification permissions are used only to deliver scheduled reminders. Calendar access is optional and is only activated when you explicitly choose to add an appointment to your device calendar. The app does not read or access your existing calendar events.

Your Control Over Your Data

You can edit, delete, or completely remove all of your data at any time via Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the application also removes all associated data from your device.

Data Retention

Your information persists only on your device until you delete it or remove the app. We hold no copies of it, because the app never sends it to us. The only exception is the web server access logs described under Sharing, which our hosting provider keeps for a short period as a routine part of serving a website, and which we do not use.

Children's Privacy

Ahead does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The app requires no account and transmits nothing on its own. The only data that leaves the device is a share link that someone deliberately creates and sends.
